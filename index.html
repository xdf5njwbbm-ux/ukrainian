Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting.

Is this a real tutor or AI?➤
It is an advanced AI model designed to simulate a native Ukrainian tutor. It is available 24/7, responds instantly, and never gets tired!
What makes this different from other apps?➤
Most apps focus on matching words. We focus on speaking and conversation practice, which is the key to fluency.
How do AI calls and texting work?➤
You can start a voice call or text chat directly in the browser. The AI responds in real-time with natural Ukrainian audio and text.
Will I have access to learning resources?➤
Yes, along with the AI tutor, you get access to grammar guides, vocabulary lists, and cultural tips.
Can I choose my own conversation topics?➤
Yes! You can talk about anything you like, or let the AI guide you through structured lessons and scenarios.
Do you offer a free trial?➤
Yes! We offer a risk-free trial so you can experience the power of AI tutoring before you commit.
Will the AI correct my mistakes?➤
Absolutely. The AI provides gentle, instant corrections on pronunciation, grammar, and vocabulary usage to help you improve fast.
Does it work on mobile?➤
Yes, the platform is fully responsive and works great on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
Can I cancel anytime?➤
Yes, there are no long-term commitments. You can cancel your subscription at any time with one click.
Is this suitable for kids and adults?➤
Yes, the AI adapts its language and topics to be appropriate and engaging for learners of all ages.
